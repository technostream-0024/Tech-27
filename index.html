About the Event

A single board, three branches feeding in

Technostream is the technical event of Dr. Panjabrao Deshmukh Polytechnic, Amravati. Students from Computer Engineering, Civil Engineering and Electronics Engineering compete in branch-specific and open technical events designed to test applied knowledge, speed and problem-solving under pressure.
